# Design

Add optional candidate feasibility evidence, populated at the calculation site (free/required/deficit units, configured bank policy, required/remaining actions and seconds). Decimal estimates preserve conservative rounding. Missing evidence in old checkpoints remains null; policy identity and discovery version are unchanged because selection is unchanged.

Use one operator projection for HTTP Inspect and checkpoint summaries, capped at 128 candidates, excluding commands, catalogs, arbitrary assumptions and resource payloads. Keep coordinator's internal decision unchanged. The browser renders textContent and never parses codes to recover numeric facts. Inspect diagnostics have their own receipt-time label, independent of persisted observation freshness. Render three alternatives with the rest expandable; unknown codes are readable technical fallback.
