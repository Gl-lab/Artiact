# R13: Needs-driven recovery

## Why
Discovery currently values gathering milestones but cannot derive a useful production chain from observed HP loss. Existing consumables require a manually named item/combat parent.

## Scope
Add an optional Recovery policy to autonomous profiles. Discover a single recovery need below a policy threshold, compare permitted rest, usable inventory/bank food and finite production paths, choose the item from supported catalog effects, and retain this parent until full HP or cancellation/infeasibility. Reuse cooking/fishing prerequisites, production capacity and atomic use verification. Persist recovery provenance and global allowances without rewarding nested materials.

## Non-goals
Combat utility, arbitrary item effects, new professions, automatic permission expansion, live gameplay and indefinite stock replenishment.
