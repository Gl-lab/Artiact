# Requirements

## Requirement: Independent useful parent
An enabled HP deficit SHALL generate a recovery parent without manual item/combat goals. Full HP SHALL generate no recovery goal. The system SHALL select supported food from catalog and compare permitted rest/stock/production using the complete path. Parent utility SHALL NOT be counted again for ingredients or training.

## Requirement: Permission and conservation
Use, craft, rest and bank withdrawals SHALL respect explicit policy, stock floors and durable global use/material limits. A rejected or unknown dispatch SHALL NOT refund charges. Shared ingredients SHALL be counted once within the selected chain; unselected alternatives SHALL reserve nothing.

## Requirement: Observable completion
The deterministic training scenario SHALL reach cooking/fishing prerequisites, produce limited meals and confirm HP20 with exact conservation through real clients, without Fight. Full recovery or parent cancellation SHALL stop refill. Lost replies SHALL reconcile the original parent without repeated POST. Unsupported/cyclic/unaffordable paths SHALL explain rejection.

## Requirement: Regression quality
R12 scenarios SHALL continue to meet all useful outcomes and zero-regression ceilings with Recovery absent. R13 comparisons SHALL preserve losses/amendments and distinguish synthetic evidence from live compatibility.
