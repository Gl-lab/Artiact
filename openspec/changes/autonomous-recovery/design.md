# Design

## User policy
`Portfolio:Recovery` is optional and only legal with AutonomousGoals. Fields: HpBelowPercent (default 50), AllowUse/AllowCraft/AllowRest/AllowBankWithdrawal (all false), MaxUsed (10), MaxMaterialUnits (100), Reserved stock floors (empty). Positive finite limits and threshold 1–100 are required. Withdrawal additionally requires the existing explicit BankRetain profile. No per-item code/value is configured. R11/R12 behavior and identity remain unchanged when Recovery is absent. Internal derived policies may enable only the user's allowed preparation/production subset; they are not new user permissions.

## Discovery and utility
One recovery parent covers the observed HP deficit, activated strictly below threshold and retained to full HP. Candidates are at most 32 ordinal consumables with exactly one positive heal effect and no conditions, plus allowed Rest. Production requires finite reachable full recipe paths and the existing supported cooking/fishing prerequisites. Unknown/cyclic/unreachable candidates are rejected before a command. Unsupported recipes remain alternatives with explanations. Healing value is 4 * initial deficit / max HP (normalized 0–4); ingredient, stock, training and use do not receive separate rewards. Existing gathering alternatives compete by full-path cost until a parent is selected.

Required quantity is ceil(deficit/heal) plus explicit retained food. Observe current inventory and only permitted bank withdrawals. Compare rest (ceil(missing HP percent), minimum 3 seconds), ready use (3 seconds plus bank delivery), and finite supply/full-path estimates with disclosed training uncertainty. Rest wins ties. R8a's wrapper gets a recovery parent and threshold 100 while active; a completed parent suppresses all refill. Ready bank food is withdrawn in the required bounded quantity when capacity permits. Cancellation releases only computed parent demand; conservative dispatched charges never refund.

## State and contracts
Recovery discovery evidence uses Skill=recovery, Target=max HP and Unlock=selected food or rest. The current algorithm remains discovery-v1 for gathering; Recovery policy is part of stable identity. Add a recovery-specific evidence version recovery-v1. Active parent/world/history remain in version-2 autonomous checkpoints; historical gathering checkpoints without Recovery keep working. A completed recovery transition records its observed HP so later damage does not erase historical completion.

Charges map to global recovery:use and recovery:materials allowances across item alternatives. Reconstruction projects those counters into the existing consumable wrapper's per-item view and remaps commands back to global charges before intent. Nested recipes reserve shared ingredients once through the existing production planner. Candidate alternatives describe the same parent need; they are not simultaneous independently rewarded food goals. Read/validate HP only when Recovery is enabled. Probe use/rest/craft/withdraw schemas only as required by enabled permissions. Add HP as the recovery path's measured useful outcome; supply actions cannot learn healing progress.
