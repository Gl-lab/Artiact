# Quality requirements

The offline gate SHALL verify preregistered useful outcomes, costs and stock on identical states for three policies with repeatable execution. Equivalent outcomes SHALL respect zero command/cooldown/movement regression against both baselines unless an advance documented tradeoff exists. At least one measured command win without cooldown growth SHALL exist.

Operational refusal, catalog changes, unknown outcomes and process restart SHALL remain distinguishable from normal completion. Neither parent renewal nor restart SHALL refund dispatched actions/materials or replay uncertain POSTs. Reports SHALL preserve missing outcome/fact coverage and per-skill XP instead of presenting summed XP as universal quality.

The delivered live protocol SHALL remain separate from offline acceptance and SHALL NOT claim live execution or lift the combat ADR. A known unresolved intermittent issue SHALL be disclosed, not silently called fixed.
