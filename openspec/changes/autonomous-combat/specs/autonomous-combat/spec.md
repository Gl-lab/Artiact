# Requirements

- Ordered stages MUST stop at their target and advance using current observed level; all stages end at the portfolio target.
- Selection MUST explain supported opponent and single-slot improvement, cost and conservative HP bound. Unknown mechanics MUST NOT produce a fight command.
- Missing equipment MUST use bounded skill/material/bank preparation. Equipment and supply MUST remain tied to the unfinished combat parent.
- Weapon and shield projection MUST preserve unrelated stats/stock, reject unknown effects and invalid subtraction, and be checked against fresh execution results.
- A deterministic real-client local acceptance MUST cross two levels with a required upgrade, recovery and terminal restart without extra actions. Independent arithmetic/stock oracles MUST accompany it.
- Negative checks MUST include inaccessible/unknown opponent, unsupported gear, impossible preparation, defeat, bad equipment response and lost response without replay.
